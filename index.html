        Think and talkwith a partner · about 10 minutes

        Show your partner your world. Talk about these questions:

        1. Would you like to live in your world? Why or why not?
        2. Which prices did you both pay? Which price was too high for you?
        3. Would everybody be happy in your world? Think of different people: a teenager, an old person, an artist, a .
        4. Who the cameras, the experts and the pills? What could go wrong?
        Useful phrases
        • I paid the price for … because …
        • For me, … was too high a price.
        • I see your point, but …
        • What would happen if …?

        Is your world still perfect?

        Be ready to explain your answer to the class.

        Writeon your own · about 10 minutes

        Would you like to live in your perfect world? Write 4–6 sentences. Use at least one if-clause.

        If-clauses (type 2): things that are not real

        If + simple past, … would + verb

        If I lived in my world, I would feel safe.

        If I would live in my world, …
